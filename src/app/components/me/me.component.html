Hi, I'm Raphaël, your friendly neighbourhood software developer and the person behind DigimezZo, the home for the software I create and share, including Dopamine, Knowte and Vitomu. The name is short for "Digital intermezzo", a little break in the digital world.

I've been passionate about software ever since I got my first computer as a teenager. It was only a matter of time before I started writing software myself, and I've been doing so for more than 20 years. I work on my projects in my spare time alongside my full-time job, simply because I enjoy creating software.

I believe software should work with you, not against you. My goal is to create applications that are clear, easy to use and stay out of your way, without unnecessary interruptions or nagging. You should be able to focus on what you want to do and enjoy doing it.

Thanks for stopping by, and I hope you enjoy using my software!
