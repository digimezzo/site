Hi, I'm Raphaël, your friendly neighbourhood software developer and the creator of Dopamine, Knowte and Vitomu. I share my projects under the name DigimezZo, short for "Digital intermezzo", a little break in the digital world.

My passion for software began when I got my first computer as a teenager. That curiosity eventually led me to create software of my own, and I've been doing so for more than 20 years. I create and work on these projects in my spare time, simply because I enjoy making software.

I believe software should work with you, not against you. My goal is to create applications that are clear, easy to use and stay out of your way, without unnecessary interruptions or nagging. You should be able to focus on what you want to do and enjoy doing it.

Thanks for stopping by, and I hope you enjoy using my software! ♥️
